# My Favorite Movie: **You Are the Apple of My Eye**

**"You Are the Apple of My Eye"** is one of my all-time favorites. It's a beautiful story about young love, friendship, and the emotional ups and downs of being a teenager.

### Plot Summary
The story revolves around **Goo Jin-woo** (played by **Jung Jin-young**) and **Oh Seon-ah** (played by **Dahyun**), two high school classmates who experience the complexities of growing up together. While the movie has its fair share of comedy and lighthearted moments, it also dives deep into the bittersweet realities of love and growing up.

### Main Cast
- **Goo Jin-woo** – Jung Jin-young
- **Oh Seon-ah** – Dahyun

### Supporting Cast
- **Son Jeong-hyuk**
- **Kim Yo-han**
- **Lee Min-goo**
- **Lee Seung-jun**
- **Kim Min-ju**

### Themes of the Movie
- **Teenage Love and Heartbreak**: The movie does a great job showing the excitement and confusion of falling in love during your school years, and the inevitable heartache that comes with it.
- **Friendship and Growth**: It emphasizes the importance of friendships that shape who we are as people and how these bonds change over time.
- **Nostalgia and Youth**: It’s also very nostalgic for anyone who’s been through high school and can relate to the carefree yet intense emotions of that time.

### Why I Chose This Movie
I chose **"You Are the Apple of My Eye"** because it feels so relatable. It’s not just about the love story, but also about the little things that make growing up such an emotional journey. Plus, the cast delivers amazing performances that really bring the characters to life. I think every student can find a piece of themselves in this film—whether it’s dealing with school, love, or simply figuring out who we are.

If you like movies that explore relationships and emotions in a heartfelt way, I highly recommend giving it a watch!
